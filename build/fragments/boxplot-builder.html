Try this first

Select journey 25 (68 min) and slide it down. Watch the dashed upper fence: it does not move. At the moment the value drops to 46 or below, it stops being an outlier and the whisker jumps out to reach it.

Then this

Slide the same journey up to 130. The orange mean triangle follows it; the median line, the box and both fences do not move at all. That is what resistant means. The SD grows too, so it is not resistant either.

And the catch

Reset, then pick a value in the middle: journey 20 (27 min). Slide it down to 14. The median drops to 17, Q3 to 24.5, and the upper fence moves in to 42.5. Resistant means the median and IQR ignore how extreme the extremes are, not that they never change.

The convention used here

Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves. When n is odd the overall median is left out of both halves, which is what a TI-84 does. An outlier is a value more than 1.5 × IQR beyond a quartile, or more than 2 SD from the mean; a value exactly on a fence is not an outlier.
